Privacy Policy

Cash or Installments? — Android application

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Developer: Mauricio Montanha Junior

This Privacy Policy explains how Cash or Installments? (the “App”), developed by Mauricio Montanha Junior (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”), handles information when you use the App. The App helps compare paying for a purchase upfront or in installments. The core calculations do not require an account.

Important summary
  • Simulation inputs, calculation results, history, favorites, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. The App does not send simulation details to the developer's server or synchronize them through Firebase.
  • The coupon and invitation features use Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication and store coupon-related records in Cloud Firestore.
  • Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play Billing may process data as described below.

1. Information handled by the App

1.1. Simulation information stored on your device

When you use the calculator, you may enter product prices, upfront discounts, installment amounts and counts, down payments, card interest rates, investment-return estimates, and early-installment settlement discounts. The App also stores calculation results, history entries, favorites, scenario names, currency-display preferences, and other app settings locally using Room and DataStore.

This information is used to perform the calculations and provide the App's history, favorites, comparisons, and reports. According to the App's current implementation, these simulation details are not sent to the developer's servers and are not stored in Firebase. Android device backup behavior, if enabled on your device, is controlled by your Android and Google account settings.

1.2. Coupon and invitation information

If you redeem a coupon or publish/share an invitation code, the App connects your Google account through Firebase Authentication for that action. Information processed for this feature may include:

  • the email address associated with your Google account and a Firebase Authentication user identifier (UID);
  • the invitation or coupon code involved in the operation;
  • coupon redemption and eligibility records, including information needed to determine whether a reward applies and how many benefit days are granted.

This information is used only to operate the invitation program, associate a redemption with the correct account, award benefits, enforce the program's rules, and help prevent duplicate, self-referred, or abusive redemptions. The App does not require Google sign-in for ordinary calculations, history, or favorites.

1.3. Install-referrer information

Invitation links may include a referral code in the Google Play install referrer parameter. When the App is first opened after installation, it can read that parameter through the Google Play Install Referrer API to identify the invitation code and apply the appropriate referral flow. The parameter is provided by Google Play; the App does not use this feature to access your browsing history.

1.4. Analytics and diagnostics

The App integrates Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Depending on the SDK configuration and your use of the App, these services may process app events and interactions (such as launches, screen or feature interactions), app and operating-system versions, device and installation identifiers, diagnostic information, crash reports, non-fatal errors, and app-not-responding (ANR) reports. This information is used to understand App usage, monitor stability, investigate errors, and improve performance. The developer does not intend to place the values you enter in the calculator, your simulation history, or your favorites in analytics or crash reports.

1.5. Advertising information

The free version displays ads through Google AdMob, including banner and interstitial or rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share data such as your IP address, ad or app-set identifiers, information about ad and app interactions (including ad views, taps, and video views), and diagnostic or performance information. An approximate general location may be inferred from an IP address. This data supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where enabled and permitted, ad personalization.

Ad personalization and available controls can depend on your region, applicable law, Android settings, and Google's settings. You can review Google's privacy and advertising controls using the links in Section 4.

1.6. Pro subscription information

Pro subscriptions are purchased and managed through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and handles payment credentials. The App receives the purchase or subscription status needed to determine whether Pro benefits should be enabled. The developer does not receive your full credit-card number through the App's Google Play Billing integration.

2. How information is used

Information is processed for the following purposes:

  • to calculate and display payment comparisons and maintain local history, favorites, and preferences;
  • to authenticate actions involving coupon redemption or publishing an invitation, validate eligibility, and award the corresponding benefits;
  • to manage Pro subscription status through Google Play;
  • to serve and measure advertising, help prevent invalid ad activity, understand app usage, diagnose errors, maintain security, and improve reliability;
  • to respond to support, privacy, or deletion requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

The App does not connect to a currency-exchange API or a financial institution and does not access bank accounts. Selecting BRL, USD, EUR, or GBP changes only how amounts are displayed; it does not convert currencies.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

5.1. Data stored locally

Simulation history, favorites, results, and preferences remain in the App's local storage until you delete the relevant entries or remove the App's local data. The developer cannot remotely delete data that exists only on your device. Android backup and restore settings may affect whether local app data is retained or restored by your device account.

5.2. Coupon account and records

Firebase Authentication and Firestore records are retained while needed to operate and secure the coupon and invitation program. You can request deletion of the account information and associated coupon records using the Account and Data Deletion page or by emailing montanhajr.dev@gmail.com. After verifying the request, the developer will process it within 30 days. Limited information may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary to resolve security, fraud-prevention, or legal issues; any retained information will be limited to that purpose.

5.3. Analytics, crash, and advertising data

Retention of information collected by Google Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, and Google Play is also governed by each service's settings, retention controls, and policies. The retention period may vary by service and type of information. You can review the provider information in Section 4 for details and available controls.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose not to use the coupon and invitation features; ordinary calculations do not require Google sign-in. You can delete individual local history entries and favorites using the App, or clear the App's local data through Android settings. Uninstalling the App stops its normal operation but does not itself delete information already processed by Google or Firebase.

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on the processing of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise a right, ask a privacy question, or make a deletion request, contact montanhajr.dev@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Do not email passwords or other authentication secrets.

Deleting App account data does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel a subscription separately through Google Play. Deleting Firebase coupon data does not remotely erase local simulation history on your device.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled by the App. Google and Firebase provide security features for their services, including encrypted data transmission where supported, authentication controls, and access rules. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

The App is a general-purpose payment-comparison utility and is not specifically designed or marketed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the coupon or invitation feature, contact us at montanhajr.dev@gmail.com so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.
