Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of information associated with the coupon and invitation features of Cash or Installments?, an Android application developed by Mauricio Montanha Junior.

What can be deleted

After verifying a request, the developer will delete the Firebase Authentication account information and associated Cloud Firestore coupon, invitation, and redemption records that can be linked to that account. A limited record may be retained only where required by law or reasonably necessary to address security, fraud-prevention, or legal issues.

How to submit a request

Send an email to montanhajr.dev@gmail.com with the subject Account and data deletion request — Cash or Installments?.

Please include:
  • the Google account email address used for the App's coupon or invitation feature;
  • your invitation/coupon code, if available;
  • confirmation that you are requesting deletion of the App account and associated coupon data.

Do not include your Google password, payment-card information, or other authentication secrets.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account. We will process verified deletion requests within 30 days and will reply to confirm completion or explain any lawful limitation.

What this request does not automatically delete

  • Local simulations: calculation results, history, favorites, and preferences are stored on your device, not in the coupon database. Delete these in the App or clear its local data through Android settings.
  • Google Play subscriptions: deleting account or coupon data does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in Google Play.
  • Data held independently by Google: Google may retain information processed under its own services and policies. Use the relevant Google privacy tools for requests concerning information that Google controls independently.
